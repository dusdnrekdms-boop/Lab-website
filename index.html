Biological Adaptability Lab

Bioinspired Soft Robotics and Electronics for Adaptive Biomedical Systems

We aim to realize bioinspired soft robots and electronic devices that can be implanted with minimal tissue damage and adapt to diverse tissues and organs for diagnosis and therapy. To achieve this goal, we develop core technologies in locomotion, sensor development and integration, and shape reconfiguration.

Research

Our lab develops bioinspired soft robots and electronics for safe biointegration in diagnosis and therapy, with a focus on locomotion, sensing and integration, and shape reconfiguration. These advances can also be applied to broader fields, including robotics and displays.

/ 01

Locomotion

Understanding the fundamental principles of biological locomotion and its interactions with the environment to develop locomotion strategies and artificial muscles that enable effective movement across diverse environments.

Science Advances (2025), TBD
/ 02

Sensing and Integration

Developing flexible electronic sensors and integrating them into robotic, electronic, and medical devices to monitor environmental and physiological states in real time, enabling adaptive responses to dynamic conditions.

Science Robotics (2021), Nature Electronics (2024)
/ 03

Shape Reconfiguration

Developing adaptive systems inspired by biological shape reconfiguration that transform their shapes to perform different functions in response to changing environments and demands.

Materials Horizons (2025), npj Flexible Electronics (2025)
